Location rule

Visits whose city is on this list are left out. A place is not proof of an own visit: it also hides strangers from the same town, and city data from an IP address is approximate. Case, spaces and a district in brackets are ignored – “Wasserburg am Inn” also covers “Wasserburg am Inn (Gabersee)”.
