Next page is the page opened directly after the article; later in the visit counts visits that reached an offer or the contact page at any point after the article. 3 · LinkedIn posts and website visits

A visit belongs to a post only through the post's tracking link (utm_content=post-<id>) – never because it happened soon after a post. LinkedIn's own clicks (Statistics → Clicks) are a different measure: a click is not a website visit (the page may not load, the visitor may block tracking, one person may click twice).
