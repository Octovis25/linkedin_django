Pages by month

One row per page, one column per month. Choose what the cells show; point at a cell for all three numbers.

  • Unique visitors – different people (Matomo's visitor id) who opened the page that month; five visits by one person count once.
  • Returning – of those, the ones who came on a visit Matomo marks as returning.
  • ⌀ time on page – average of the measured views. The last page of a visit has no end time and is not measured; a cell without any measurement shows “–”, not 0.
  • From the visit log, counted visits only (rules and exclusions on Visitors). Months in Europe/Berlin. Without a date range the last six months; at most twelve.
