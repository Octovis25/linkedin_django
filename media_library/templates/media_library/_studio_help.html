🎨 Image Studio – User Guide

Build images, GIFs and videos for your posts. The left column holds the tools, the canvas is in the middle, and uploads, assets and your finished outputs are on the right.

⭐ Fill a cut-out (transparent) background

  1. In the Canvas panel, go to the Colour row.
  2. Click Cream, White, a Video-Teal (for video/GIF), or the small colour box for any custom colour.
  3. The whole canvas fills instantly; your elements stay on top.
  4. Back to see-through: click Transparent.

If a template loaded a background image (e.g. a teal layout), a colour sits behind it and stays hidden – click 🚫 Remove background image first, then pick your colour.

The panels (left column, top to bottom)

  • Templates – reusable layouts. New template, Save template, + Manage.
  • Canvas – Size, Clear, the Colour row (background fill), Image as background, Remove background image.
  • Text – + Text, + Text block (heading + body), ✓ Check + text, ✓✓✓ Triple check.
  • Shapes – arrows, lines, circles, rectangles, hexagons, and Circle / Hexagon / Banner + text.
  • Colour – colour for the selected text/shape and new shapes, plus the lighter Video-Teals for video/GIF.
  • Cut out & retouch – see below.

Top & bottom bars

  • Bar above the canvas: ↶ Undo / ↷ Redo (undo the last action – Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y), plus Grid, Zoom, Large and alignment tools.
  • Bar below the canvas: 💾 Save as…, ▾ Format, 📥 Download, and ← Exit (leave the Studio and go back to the overview).

Right column

  • Upload – Upload image or Import SVG (tick “insert as 1 object” to keep an SVG whole).
  • Assets – tick one or more folders (or a parent) to show their images; search within the selection.
  • My outputs – your finished Images / GIFs / Videos. Click to reopen, ✕ to delete.

🛠 Step by step: build a post

  1. Start from a template. Click a thumbnail under Templates (or New template for a blank one). The background and logo load onto the canvas.
  2. Set the background. In Canvas → Colour pick Cream or White – or a Video-Teal if you’ll export a GIF/video.
  3. Add your image. Use Upload image on the right, or tick a folder under Assets and click a picture. Drag it into place; scale from a corner handle.
  4. Cut it out (optional). Select the image, then Cut out & retouch → Remove background automatically. Touch up with Erase / Restore, or recolour an area with Recolour area.
  5. Add text. + Text block for a heading + body, or ✓✓✓ Triple check for a checklist. Double-click to edit; change the colour in the Colour panel.
  6. Add shapes/badges (optional). From Shapes: an arrow, a Circle + text, or a Banner + text to highlight something.
  7. Animate (optional). Select an element; in the bar under the canvas choose a Motion or Effect and a Speed, then ▶ Preview.
  8. Save. Type a Title, then 💾 Save as… and choose Image (PNG), GIF or Video. It attaches to the open post and also appears under My outputs.

Tip: keep it to one clear message – a heading, a supporting line, one image and (at most) one highlight.

Concrete example (like the screenshot):
  1. Open a template → the cream background + Octotrial logo load.
  2. Tick the Logo folder in Assets, click the octopus figure, place it bottom-right, scale from the corner.
  3. If it has a background: select it → Remove background automatically.
  4. + Text block → heading “Trust series with responsibility”, add a short body line.
  5. ✓✓✓ Triple check → type your three bullet lines.
  6. Set the text colour (petrol) in the Colour panel.
  7. Title it, 💾 Save as… → Image (PNG). Done.

🎬 Make a GIF or video

  1. Build the post first (image + text), exactly as above.
  2. Add movement. Select an element; in the bar under the canvas set a Motion (e.g. Fade in, In from bottom) and/or an Effect (e.g. Network, Confetti), plus a Speed. Repeat for other elements.
  3. Preview. Click ▶ Preview to play it on the canvas.
  4. Length. Set Video length (seconds) in the bar; empty = automatic. Looping effects run a few seconds.
  5. Colour tip. Export darkens the background a little – choose a Video-Teal as the background colour to compensate.
  6. Save. 💾 Save as… → GIF (smaller, autoplays in the feed) or Video (WebM, better for longer clips). It attaches to the post and appears under My outputs → GIFs / Videos.

Cut out & retouch (an image)

  • Automatic: Remove background automatically, or Remove checkerboard only.
  • Make transparent (click): Remove colour everywhere, Remove area.
  • Recolour by clicking – one tool, no colour palette: click 🎨 Recolour area (or 🎨 Recolour whole colour), then first click the colour you want anywhere (e.g. your petrol background – it shows in the little swatch), and then click the area(s) in the image to recolour. “Recolour area” affects only the connected area you click; “whole colour” affects every matching area (e.g. all books, a piece of clothing). Pick the background colour to make part of the image blend into the background. To use a new colour, press the tool button again.
  • Wipe with the brush: Erase, Restore, Paint.
  • Select an area, then apply: Drag rectangle or Free mark, then Delete or Recolour.
  • These edits are kept on save – also inside templates.

Editing an existing template

  1. Click + Manage under Templates, then Open in editor.
  2. Make your changes.
  3. Click 💾 Save template → choose Update this template.
  4. Keep the same name – a new name creates a copy.

Saving to a post

  • 💾 Save as… attaches the image to the open post.
  • ▾ Format – save as image (PNG), GIF or video.
  • Each post holds one medium – a new one replaces the old.

Connect a Studio image with a post’s text

You write the text in the post, and link the image in one of two ways:

  • Image already made in the Studio: in Create Posts → Planner, open/edit the post, open the 📷 from Studio… dropdown at the image field and pick your finished image (from My outputs). It attaches to that post; text and image now belong together and go out together. (Videos: the 🎨 Studio outputs group in the video dropdown.)
  • Start from the post: click Open Studio on the post → the Studio opens linked to it. When you 💾 Save as…, the image attaches to that post automatically – no picking needed.

Troubleshooting

  • Change not showing? Press Ctrl + Shift + R (reload without cache).
  • Colour too dark in video/GIF? Use a Video-Teal as the background colour.
