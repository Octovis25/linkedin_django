🎨 Image Studio – User Guide

⭐ Fill a cut-out (transparent) background

  1. In the Canvas panel on the left, find the row “Hintergrundfarbe” (Background colour).
  2. Click a colour: Creme, Weiß (White), one of the lighter Video-Teals (for video/GIF), or the small colour-picker box on the far left for any custom colour.
  3. The whole canvas fills instantly; your elements stay on top.
  4. To go transparent again, click “Transparent”.

A background colour and a background image replace each other – setting one removes the other.

Two kinds of “cut-out”

  • One image: select it → “BG weg” (Remove background) removes that image’s own background.
  • The whole canvas: use “Hintergrundfarbe”, “Bild als Hintergrund” (Image as background) and “Hintergrundbild entfernen” (Remove background image).

Editing an existing template

  1. Click “+ Verwalten” (Manage) under Templates.
  2. Next to the template, click “Im Editor öffnen” (Open in editor).
  3. Make your changes.
  4. Click “💾 Vorlage speichern” (Save template) → choose “Diese Vorlage aktualisieren” (Update this template).
  5. Keep the same name – a new name creates a copy.

Text

  • “+ Text” – single text field. “+ Textblock” – heading + body as one object.
  • “✓ Haken + Text” (Check + text) and “✓✓✓ Dreihaken” (Triple check) for checkmark lines.
  • Double-click text to edit; change colour via the box in the Text panel.

Editing an image

  • Cut out / “BG weg” – make the background transparent.
  • Recolour / “Farbtausch” – pick a colour, click the area to recolour (shading is preserved).
  • These edits are kept on save – also inside templates.

Saving & My outputs

  • “💾 Speichern” (Save) attaches the image to the open post.
  • “▾ Format” – save as image (PNG), GIF or video.
  • Under “Meine Ausgaben” (My outputs): click to reopen, ✕ to delete.

Troubleshooting

  • Change not showing? Press Ctrl + Shift + R (reload without cache).
  • Colour too dark in video/GIF? Use a Video-Teal as the background colour.
